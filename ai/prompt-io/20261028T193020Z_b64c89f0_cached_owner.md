## Contract and implementation

docs/guide/asyncio.rst already states that the default
suppress_graceful_exits=True absorbs TaskExited signals. A reduced
Tractor-only test reproduces the violation without IB or Piker.
No historical first-bad Tractor commit has been established.

LinkedTaskChannel._aio_owner_exit records the exact object issued
by open_channel_from() at owner exit. The done callback consumes
that echo instead of cancelling its owner and raising it again.
The translator preserves genuine Trio errors, suppresses normal
echoes by default, and reports the same object under explicit policy.
Type-only suppression is deliberately avoided; cleanup errors and
newly constructed TrioTaskExited instances retain propagation.
